**Vice Panels** is a GTA-style cover art maker that runs entirely offline in the browser. Load your own photos (file picker or drag and drop — they never leave your machine), arrange them across four multi-panel poster layouts (six-panel grid, hero + strips, 2×2 quad, side hero) with crisp white borders, crop each panel by dragging to pan and scrolling or sliding to zoom, wash the collage in a Vice City sunset, neon night, teal/orange or B&W noir colour grade, and finish it with bold display lettering — a title across the top and a caption along the foot, set in the vendored OFL-licensed Anton face. One click exports the finished cover as a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Five bundled sample scenes (three drawn for this app — a sunset boulevard, a neon pier with an airboat, a skyline with a helicopter — plus two make.fun starter photos) fill the panels on first open, so the maker works before you add a photo of your own. Plain static files under `app/`, ES modules, no build step, no network requests. Original homage only: no Rockstar logos, artwork, characters or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
# hand the entry in with: mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
